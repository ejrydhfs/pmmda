1. Introduction
---------------
   
The pmMDA application and the used frameworks should be tested automatically to
insure quality of the delivered code.

2. Test Scenarios
-----------------

2.1 Business object graph Test (create, read, update, delete)
  The test verifies the behavior of the business object framework including  
  lightweight objects, the transport layer and the persistence framework.
	- Create a hierarchy of business objects, store them in the database and
	  retrieve them. 
	- Modify the hierarchy of business objects, store and retrieve them.
	- Delete the hierarchy of business objects, store and retrieve them.
